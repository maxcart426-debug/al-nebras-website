What We Build

One team, every aluminium & glass system your villa needs.

From the front gate to the garden room, every piece is designed, cut and installed by our own crew — no subcontracting, no guesswork.

01 — Pergolas & Car Parking Shades

Shade structures built for your exact terrace or driveway.

Laser-cut hexagon and louvered panels, sized to your space and fixed to withstand UAE sun and wind — for gardens, terraces and covered parking alike.

02 — Glass Rooms & Sunrooms

Turn an unused corner of the villa into a room you'll actually use.

Frameless sliding glass and slim aluminium profiles bring in light without losing the view — built as an extension of the house, not an add-on.

03 — Glass Partitions & Shower Enclosures

Frameless glass, fitted precisely to every bathroom.

Shower screens, tub enclosures and glass partitions — cut and hinged on site so every panel sits flush, with no gaps and no leaks.

04 — Kitchen Cabinets & Joinery

Full kitchen fit-outs, from carcass to countertop.

Custom cabinetry built and installed in-house, matched to your kitchen's exact layout and finished to the same standard as our glass and aluminium work.
